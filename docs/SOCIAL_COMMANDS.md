# Social MCP commands

Planned read commands include `social_list_brands`, `social_list_accounts`, `social_list_posts`, `social_get_analytics`, `social_get_comments`, `social_campaign_performance`, `social_content_opportunities`, and `social_rank_content`.

Draft-only workflows include product-linked campaign generation and platform adaptation. The default mode is REVIEW. Public publishing, scheduling, deletion, and autonomous replies are disabled until each platform's official OAuth, account identity, readback, and capability review are complete.

Supported content pillars for MOTORYN are `UPGRADE YOUR CAR`, `CLEAN YOUR CAR`, and `BE READY ON THE ROAD`.
